# The Chat Place 0.1.4

An update to 0.1.3, still a pre-release. If you have 0.1.3 installed, it offers this update itself
(Help, Check for Updates asks at once), and your sessions and settings are kept.

## Changed

- **Usage and Context is now a list you can read.** View, Usage and Context (Ctrl+Shift+U) now
  opens a list: the loaded session's context first ("Context 62% full: 124,000 of 200,000
  tokens"), then each usage limit on a line of its own, such as the 5-hour limit and the weekly
  limit, with when each resets. Arrow through it at your own pace, or read it on a braille
  display, instead of having to catch it in a voice you may not be able to follow. Ctrl+C or Copy
  (Alt+C) copies the selected line, Copy All (Alt+A) copies every line, and Escape closes the
  list. (#130, #137)

## Fixed

- **On Windows, two spoken confirmations close together are both heard.** When two came within a
  moment of each other, such as "Sent" and then the session's state, the second could replace the
  first, so you heard the second one twice, or the first not at all. Each is now spoken in turn.
  (#113, #135)
- **Send Now no longer leaves a turn stuck, cuts an answer short, or hides an error.** A queued
  message sent with Send Now (Ctrl+Enter) could leave the turn waiting for up to ten minutes and
  then report success when the answer had in fact failed. A long answer to it could be cut off
  after ten minutes, so Claude's permission requests after that couldn't be answered. If Claude
  finished what it was doing at the moment you pressed Send Now, the turn could end with that
  earlier answer instead of waiting for the reply to your message. And Send Now pressed in the
  first moments of a turn could reach Claude before the turn's own message. Now the turn waits for
  the answer to your Send Now message, shows its error if it fails, and sends your messages in the
  order you sent them. (#83, #136)

## Added

- **Show only the sessions that are working, idle, or have a new reply.** View, Show Sessions
  (Alt+V, H) now has Working, With a New Reply and Idle, alongside Needs You. You stay on the same
  session when it's still listed. (#134, #PR)
- **Choose what each session's line says, and in what order.** View, Session List Columns
  (Alt+V, E) lets you put the status first, so you hear "needs you", "working" or "idle" before a
  session's title, and leave out parts you don't need, such as the folder or Remote Control. Add,
  Remove, Move Up, Move Down, Move to Top, Move to Bottom and Reset to Default each say what they
  did, and a preview reads the selected session's line as it will be. In the dialog, Enter adds a
  column, Delete removes one, and Alt+Up, Alt+Down, Alt+Home and Alt+End move one (Option on a
  Mac). OK changes the list at once, and your choice is remembered. (#134, #PR)

## Reporting Issues

Found a problem or have a suggestion? Help, **Report a Bug** fills in a report and shows you
exactly what it includes before it goes anywhere: never your session titles, folders or messages.

1. **Open on GitHub** copies the report and opens a new GitHub issue with it filled in. This needs
   access to the repository.
2. **Copy Report** puts the report on the clipboard, to email to
   [support@theideaplace.net](mailto:support@theideaplace.net).

## Downloads

The same files as 0.1.3: TheChatPlace-windows-Setup.exe, TheChatPlace-windows-Portable.zip and
TheChatPlace-macos-arm64.dmg, with the update feeds. See the
[user guide](https://github.com/kellylford/AIChat/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md) to get started, and
the [README](https://github.com/kellylford/AIChat#readme) for everything The Chat Place does.

## Requirements

The same as 0.1.0: Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac with Apple silicon, and Claude Code installed with
its native installer and signed in to a Claude subscription. See the 0.1.0 release notes for the
details.
